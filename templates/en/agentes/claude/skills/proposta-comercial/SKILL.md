# Commercial proposal

## Inputs

`docs/01-visao.md` (scope and out of scope), `docs/08-backlog.md` (epics and milestones) and `docs/orcamento.md`. If `orcamento.md` does not exist or is outdated, run `{{prumo}} orcamento` first.

## Procedure

1. Write for the client, not for developers: benefits and results, no jargon.
2. **Answer what the client asked for in the proposal.** If the request lists what the proposal must include (portfolio, recommended technologies, deadline, stages, support...), each item needs a section or row. The portfolio belongs to the freelancer: leave `[FILL: links to similar projects]`.
3. **Included scope** = base scope epics; **optional** = Could stories, each with its hours, for the client to choose; **not included** = the vision's "Out of scope" section, plus common items the client may assume (hosting, domain, content, photos, monthly maintenance).
4. **Schedule** = backlog milestones, with deadline and payment percentage. The deadline considers the hours with contingency.
5. **Investment:** use the totals from `docs/orcamento.md` (base scope with contingency; optional items separately). Do not invent values. If the hourly rate is not configured, leave the calculation indicated (`N h × hourly rate`).
6. **Client's recurring costs** (hosting, paid APIs, app stores) come from `07-arquitetura.md`.
7. **Blocking questions:** list, in plain language, the questions in section 9 of the briefing marked as blocking and say what may change in the price depending on the answer.
8. **Warranty, support, scope changes and intellectual property:** use the terms the user provides. Without them, propose usual freelance terms, marked as a proposal: 30 days of defect fixes after delivery, code and rights transferred after full payment, scope changes quoted separately. Remind the user to review them (this is not legal advice).

## Output

Proposal ready for review, with gaps only in what belongs to the freelancer (hourly rate, portfolio, contact details) and an objective list of what the user needs to decide.
